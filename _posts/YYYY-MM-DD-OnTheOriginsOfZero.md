How did the number zero emerge? Popularly, the symbol '0' represents nothingness and it is surrounded by certain mysticism. However, far from the mysticism the point of this blog post is to showcase that the symbol '0', and with it the number zero, emerged not as a consequence of having a notion of nothingness, but as a consequence of the development of the positional writing system for number. In this way, zero emerges as a limiting concept of the way of writing positionally numbers.
